## Who I am

I'm **Himanshu Sharma**, an Applied Scientist at **Amazon Ads** in Bengaluru, working at the
intersection of **robotics, computer vision and machine learning**.

My research background is in robotic manipulation and vision-based control. My day job is
large-scale multimodal AI. What connects them is a single question I keep coming back to:
how do you get a learned model to act reliably in a world it was not explicitly programmed
for?

Most of my recent independent work is on **robot learning** — implementing manipulation
policies from their original papers rather than importing them, building the simulation and
teleoperation infrastructure they need, and then being honest about where they fail. The
[Projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) page has the detailed write-ups, failure
analyses included.

## Areas of interest

- Robot learning and embodied intelligence
- Imitation learning, reinforcement learning and control
- Computer vision, LLMs and vision-language models
- Robotics, motion planning and state estimation
- Retrieval-augmented and agentic AI systems

## Experience

### Applied Scientist — Amazon Ads
*January 2024 – Present*

I build and deploy large-scale deep learning systems for **content understanding**,
combining text, image and video signals to improve moderation efficiency across global
marketplaces. The work spans model development, production deployment and the operational
reality of keeping large models healthy under real traffic.

Alongside that I work on **active learning**, **representation learning** and **model
reliability** — approaches that make large models more data-efficient and more trustworthy,
which matters far more at production scale than a headline benchmark number does.

## Beyond the day job

I learn by building and then by trying to break what I built. That usually means picking a
paper, implementing it end to end, running it until it fails, and then working out precisely
*why* it failed — which has consistently taught me more than a working first attempt would
have. A fair amount of what is on this site is exactly that process, written down.

I also keep a steady stream of structured coursework going in parallel; the
[Learning]({{ '/learnings/' | relative_url }}) page lists it.
